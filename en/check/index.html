FAQ

How does the check work?

The page tries to load tiny icons from a Russian service, a regular foreign site and blocked services. What loads and what doesn't shows whether you have a whitelist, a full shutdown or a VPN problem.

How do I check for a whitelist?

Open the Whitelist tab, turn off VPN and Wi-Fi, keep mobile data and press the button. If only Yandex, VK, Mail.ru and Avito open while Google and Wikipedia don't, whitelist mode is on.

Why is the result sometimes different?

Blocking is uneven and mobile networks can be unstable. So each service is checked up to 3 times in a row and the result counts every attempt. "Unstable" means the service opened only on a retry.

Do you store my data?

No. The check runs entirely in your browser; nothing is sent to our server.

How accurate is it?

It's a quick approximate check. If the result looks odd, run it again or compare mobile data and Wi-Fi.
