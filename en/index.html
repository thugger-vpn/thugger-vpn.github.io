The Incy app (or HAPP, V2RayTun) for your device and the subscription link from the Telegram bot. Step-by-step guide is on the setup page.

Is it hard to set up?

No. Download the app, get the link in the bot and tap Connect, usually 2 minutes. If something doesn't work, support will walk you through it.

Do I need to turn on my VPN before the check?

If you want to test your VPN, yes. Without a VPN the check shows what's blocked for you right now. For the whitelist check it's the other way round: turn VPN and Wi-Fi off.

Why does the check sometimes show different results?

Blocking is uneven: the mobile network sometimes lets a connection through and sometimes cuts it, especially in the evening and during restrictions. That's why each service is checked up to 3 times and you see the overall result. "Unstable" means the VPN isn't holding steady.

What if ThuggerVPN stops working too?

Blocking changes and no VPN is perfect. If something stops opening, change the server in the app or message support: we track blocking every day and will point you to a working option.

What if it doesn't work?

Message support @ThuggerVPN_supp - a real person will reply and help pick a working server.
